# Support

Eazy Review is an independent project without a staffed support service or
guaranteed response time.

- **Setup and usage:** read the [README](README.md) and
  [development guide](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md). For a remaining question, open
  [a question issue](https://github.com/tyson-hu/Eazy-Review/issues/new?title=Question%3A%20)
  with the relevant guide section, platform and what you tried.
- **Reproducible bugs:** use the [bug report form](https://github.com/tyson-hu/Eazy-Review/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Feature ideas:** use the [feature request form](https://github.com/tyson-hu/Eazy-Review/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
  after checking [product scope](docs/BLUEBOOK.md) and existing issues.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/tyson-hu/Eazy-Review/security/advisories/new)
  and follow the [security policy](docs/SECURITY.md). Do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly.
- **Conduct concerns:** follow the private reporting route in the
  [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Public issues and screenshots must not contain credentials, environment files,
recovery links, private notes or personal account information. Share only
sanitized reproduction details.
